Tangible proof

Our milestones are backed by facts.

In aerospace engineering, results speak louder than claims. Every step forward is tested, verified, and transparently documented.

PROVEN FLIGHT 25.04.2025

PERSEVERANCE Test Flight

PERSEVERANCE successfully launched on April 25, 2025, reaching ~1 km in altitude. It stands as Cameroon's first experimental rocket designed, built, and flown locally with full telemetry transmission.

R&D STATUS 75% ACTIVE

COMORAN Demonstrator

COMORAN, our electric demonstrator, has completed 75% of its engineering and testing cycle. It validates vital onboard electronic systems and high-precision control avionics.

Our mission

Mastering space technology, step by step.

NOVA SPACE is a Cameroonian space startup founded in 2024. We develop propulsion technologies, onboard avionics, and reusability systems with the long-term vision of deploying African satellites from Cameroon.

Each program is built on incremental validation: completed accomplishments, active development, and long-term horizons are always explicitly distinguished.

01 / PROVE

Tangible Prototypes

Validate thermodynamic principles and control electronics on physical hardware before scaling.

02 / DOCUMENT

Clear Telemetry

Record and share verified data from each launch campaign with our academic and scientific partners.

03 / INSPIRE

Catalyze African Aerospace

Demonstrate that world-class space technology can be designed, manufactured, and operated directly from Cameroon.

Technological roadmap

Five programs building sovereign capability.

From laboratory propulsion testing to long-term orbital ambitions, discover the evolutionary path of our vehicles.

ACHIEVED 2024

SKY RAIDER

First propulsion demonstrator

Laboratory-scale test bench that validated our initial rocket motor chambers and baseline fluidic injection concepts.

ACHIEVED 25.04.2025

PERSEVERANCE

First flight · ~1 km altitude

Cameroon's first experimental rocket to successfully fly and transmit real-time telemetry from launch rail to apogee.

IN PROGRESS — 75% ACTIVE

COMORAN

Electric demonstrator

Currently at 75% progress. Validates advanced electric actuator control, flight software, and redundant power distribution.

OBJECTIVE FUTURE

CAMAROES

Reusability demonstrator

Technical research platform dedicated to mastering controlled deceleration and recovery technologies. A technology demonstrator, not a commercial service.

OBJECTIVE LONG-TERM

Orbital Launcher

Long-term horizon

The strategic destination of NOVA SPACE: delivering independent orbital access for African small satellites directly from Cameroon.
