Technological roadmap

Five steps to orbit.

NOVA SPACE develops its space technologies through rigorous incremental validation. Each program delivers empirical proof, never speculative promises.

PROGRAM 01 · PROPULSION BENCH
ACHIEVED

SKY RAIDER

First propulsion demonstrator

SKY RAIDER is our foundational propulsion demonstrator. It provided static bench testing to validate internal ballistics, injector atomization patterns, combustion stability, and baseline data-acquisition electronics that empower every subsequent rocket program.

Validated Capabilities

  • Experimental rocket propulsion and chamber thermodynamics
  • Static laboratory firing tests and sensor calibration
  • Proof-of-concept validation of fuel injector geometry
PROGRAM 02 · EXPERIMENTAL FLIGHT
ACHIEVED · FLIGHT TESTED

PERSEVERANCE

First experimental test flight — April 25, 2025

PERSEVERANCE is the first experimental rocket fully engineered and successfully launched in Cameroon. Designed by Victor EPOMBO EBOKO DINDE and the NOVA SPACE engineering team, its historic ascent demonstrated Cameroon's homegrown capacity to develop operational aerospace launch technology.

PROGRAM 03 · ACTIVE DEMONSTRATOR
IN PROGRESS — 75%

COMORAN

Electric flight demonstrator

COMORAN is our active electric flight demonstrator, currently at 75% engineering completion. It serves as an integrated testbed for high-speed electro-mechanical actuators, digital power governance, and redundant flight-control algorithms. COMORAN is under active development and is not a finalized program.

COMORAN Program Completion 75%

Target Capabilities

  • High-bandwidth electrical control actuation
  • Advanced redundant onboard avionics
  • Hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) integration testing
PROGRAM 04 · REUSABILITY RESEARCH
OBJECTIVE

CAMAROES

Reusability demonstrator

CAMAROES is our targeted reusability demonstrator program. Its purpose is purely experimental: developing and validating the guidance dynamics, throttled propulsive deceleration, and landing leg deployment necessary for recoverable flight stages.

Clarification: CAMAROES is an engineering technology demonstrator, not a commercial launch vehicle. Full orbital delivery is addressed in our separate, long-term roadmap.

Research Domains

  • Controlled aerodynamic descent and grid-fin steering concepts
  • Throttleable rocket engine cycle modeling
  • Precision touchdown structural dynamics
PROGRAM 05 · LONG-TERM HORIZON
LONG-TERM OBJECTIVE

Orbital Launcher

The long-term vision of NOVA SPACE

The orbital launcher represents the horizon toward which all our developmental efforts steer: establishing autonomous, sovereign orbital insertion capabilities for African nanosatellites and scientific payloads launched directly from Cameroonian soil.

In strict adherence to aerospace rigor, no orbital dates, pricing schemes, or commercial promises are established in advance of physical suborbital demonstrator certification.
